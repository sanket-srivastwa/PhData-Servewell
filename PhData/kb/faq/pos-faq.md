# POS Systems — Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**Q: What POS systems does ServeWell support?**
A: We support FoodTech POS versions 4.2.x and 5.1.x, and OrbitPOS version 2.x. Check your system's splash screen or Settings menu to confirm your version. If you're unsure which system your store uses, contact the IT Help Desk with your store location.

**Q: When should I restart the POS system?**
A: Perform a full POS restart once daily, ideally during your slowest service period (typically early morning or between lunch and dinner). A daily restart clears temporary cache, prevents memory leaks, and reduces system crashes. Always notify your team before restarting so they can complete any in-progress transactions.

**Q: Can I update my POS system software myself?**
A: No. All POS software updates must be performed by IT Support only. Unauthorized updates can corrupt your system, void warranty coverage, and cause payment processing failures. If you receive an update notification, document the version number and contact IT to schedule an update during non-peak hours.

**Q: How do I know which version of FoodTech POS we're running?**
A: On the main menu, tap **Settings** → **System Information** → **Version**. For OrbitPOS, go to **Main Menu** → **Administration** → **System Details**. Write down the full version number (e.g., "FoodTech 5.1.2") when contacting IT about issues.

## Daily Operations

**Q: How do I reload receipt paper?**
A: First, locate the paper roll compartment on the rear or side of your receipt printer. Open the access panel, remove the empty roll, insert a new roll with the paper facing forward, and close the panel until you hear a click. Run a test receipt from your POS (Settings → Print Test Receipt) to confirm proper alignment. If the printer doesn't feed paper after reloading, call IT—the print head may need cleaning.

**Q: I forgot my manager PIN. How do I reset it?**
A: You cannot reset your own PIN for security reasons. Contact your Store Manager or General Manager to submit a PIN reset request to IT Support. Provide your employee ID and the store location. IT will reset your PIN to a temporary value; you'll be prompted to create a new PIN on your next login. Allow 2–4 hours for processing during business hours.

**Q: What should I do if I get locked out of the POS after multiple failed login attempts?**
A: The system automatically locks accounts after five failed password attempts as a security measure. Wait 15 minutes for the lockout to clear, then try logging in again with your correct credentials. If you're still locked out or unsure of your password, contact IT Support—do not attempt to reset it yourself or use another employee's credentials.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: What do error codes mean, and how do I read them?**
A: POS error codes appear as numbers (e.g., "Error 2047") or alphanumeric codes (e.g., "PY-401") on your screen. Write down the complete error code, any accompanying message, and note what you were doing when it appeared. Refer to the error code reference guide posted near your registers, or contact IT with this information—it helps us diagnose issues faster than a verbal description alone.

**Q: The POS is running slowly. What can I help troubleshoot?**
A: First, check if multiple terminals are running simultaneously and close unnecessary programs on the main server. Restart the affected register (not a full system restart unless multiple registers are affected). Verify your internet connection is stable—run a speed test if available. If slowness persists after a restart, document the specific action that's slow (e.g., "report generation takes 5+ minutes") and contact IT, as this may indicate a server or network issue.

**Q: How do I handle a "Payment Declined" or payment processing error?**
A: First, ask the customer to try a different payment method (another card, cash, etc.). Then restart the payment terminal only (not the entire POS). Try the transaction again with the original payment method. If the error repeats, note the error code and contact IT—do not manually override the decline or attempt to process the payment outside the POS system, as this creates audit and compliance issues.

**Q: The receipt printer isn't printing. What should I check?**
A: Confirm the printer is powered on and has paper loaded (see paper reload instructions above). Check that the printer cable is firmly connected to the POS terminal. In Settings, select **Print Test Receipt**—if a test receipt prints, the issue is with your transaction setup, not the printer. If no test receipt prints, power cycle the printer (off for 30 seconds, then back on). If the printer still doesn't respond, contact IT.

**Q: Why is the cash drawer not opening?**
A: The drawer should open automatically when a transaction completes. If it doesn't, verify the drawer cable is connected securely to the POS terminal or receipt printer. Ensure the drawer isn't physically stuck or locked. In FoodTech, go to **Settings** → **Hardware** → **Test Drawer**; in OrbitPOS, use **Administration** → **Peripheral Test**. If the test doesn't trigger the drawer, contact IT—the drawer mechanism or driver may need replacement.

## When to Call IT Support

**Q: When should I call IT versus trying to fix a POS issue myself?**
A: **Handle yourself:** receipt paper reloads, simple restarts, checking connections. **Call IT immediately for:** payment processing failures, system crashes or freezing, login lockouts, hardware not responding (printer, drawer, scanner), persistent slowness, or any error code that doesn't resolve after a restart. When in doubt, call IT—it's faster than troubleshooting blind, and we can prevent data loss or security issues.

**Q: The entire POS system is down and affecting all registers. What do I do?**
A: Stop all transactions immediately. Do not attempt to restart the server yourself unless instructed by IT. Document the time the outage started and any error messages visible on screen. Call the IT Help Desk immediately with your store location and the system version—provide any error codes or unusual activity you noticed before the outage. In the interim, write down orders and payment details manually to process once the system recovers.

**Q: How do I report an issue to IT Support?**
A: Call the IT Help Desk during business hours (7 AM–9 PM, Monday–Friday) or raise a ticket through the Knowledge Base portal. Include your store location, POS system and version, terminal number (if applicable), the issue description, any error codes, and steps you've already taken. Photos of error messages are helpful. The more details you provide, the faster we can resolve it.
