# Soft Serve Machines — Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**Q: How often should soft serve machines be cleaned?**
A: Daily cleaning is required at the end of each shift for all models (CreamTech SC-300, SC-500, and FrostyPro 800). Perform a full deep clean weekly, including the mix hopper, dispensing valve, and interior walls. Follow the manufacturer cleaning schedule in your machine's manual, as improper cleaning can void warranty coverage.

**Q: What's the difference between "mix low" and "empty"?**
A: "Mix low" appears when the product level drops below 20% of tank capacity and means you should prepare a fresh batch soon—the machine will continue serving. "Empty" indicates 0% product remaining and the machine will stop dispensing entirely. Always refill before reaching empty to maintain service continuity during peak hours.

**Q: What temperature should soft serve mix be stored at before loading into the machine?**
A: Soft serve mix must be stored at 41°F (5°C) or below before dispensing. Never use mix that has been left at room temperature for more than 2 hours. Check storage temperatures twice daily using a calibrated thermometer and log results in your food safety records.

**Q: Can I serve soft serve if the machine displays a temperature warning?**
A: No. Stop serving immediately if any temperature alarm activates or if the display shows the product temperature is above 45°F (7°C). Contact IT support or your machine technician right away—this is a critical food safety issue and the machine must be taken offline until repaired.

**Q: Which machines require daily calibration?**
A: The FrostyPro 800 requires daily calibration of its temperature sensor before opening. The CreamTech SC-300 and SC-500 require calibration weekly. Follow the step-by-step calibration guide posted on the machine's control panel or reference your operations manual.

## Cleaning & Maintenance

**Q: What's the fastest way to clean a soft serve machine between shifts?**
A: Use the automated rinse cycle available on all three models—access it through the main menu and select "Quick Rinse" (5–7 minutes). This flushes the dispensing lines and valve but does not replace end-of-shift deep cleaning. Always perform full disassembly cleaning at least once per day.

**Q: How do I know if the mix hopper needs cleaning urgently?**
A: If you notice buildup on hopper walls, discoloration, or an off smell, stop serving and clean immediately—this indicates bacterial growth. Do not wait for the next scheduled cleaning. Isolate the machine and contact your supervisor.

**Q: Can I use bleach to clean the interior of the machine?**
A: No. Use only food-safe sanitizers approved in your brand's cleaning manual (typically quaternary ammonia or chlorine-based food-service sanitizers). Bleach can corrode seals and damage the machine's internal components. Always rinse thoroughly with potable water after sanitizing.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: What does error code E-02 mean?**
A: E-02 indicates a temperature sensor malfunction (occurs on all three models). The machine will not dispense. Power off the machine, wait 30 seconds, and restart. If the error persists after restart, take the machine offline and call IT support—the sensor may need replacement.

**Q: The machine is making a grinding noise. Is it safe to keep serving?**
A: No. Stop serving immediately and power off the machine. A grinding noise suggests a mechanical issue with the pump or auger that could contaminate product or cause equipment failure. Contact IT support or your technician before restarting.

**Q: Why is soft serve coming out too soft/runny?**
A: Check the mix temperature first—if it's above 41°F, the mix may be too warm. Verify the machine's freezing temperature setting is between 12–18°F (consult your model's control panel). If temperatures are correct, the compressor may be failing; contact IT support.

**Q: The display is frozen and the machine won't respond to button inputs.**
A: Power off the machine completely for 2 minutes, then power it back on. If the display remains frozen or unresponsive, there may be a control board issue—do not attempt further troubleshooting. Contact IT support or your equipment technician immediately.

## When to Call IT Support

**Q: When should I escalate a soft serve issue to IT instead of troubleshooting myself?**
A: Contact IT support if: the machine displays any error code other than a low-mix warning, temperature alarms activate, the machine loses power unexpectedly, you see visible damage or corrosion, or any cleaning procedure doesn't resolve the issue. Do not attempt to disassemble the machine yourself.

**Q: My machine stopped dispensing but there's mix in the tank. What do I do?**
A: First, verify the mix level sensor isn't stuck (lightly tap the hopper). If mix is present, restart the machine. If dispensing doesn't resume within 1 minute, the pump or valve may be jammed—power off and call IT support. Do not attempt to force the dispensing lever.

**Q: Is there a log I should keep for soft serve machine maintenance?**
A: Yes. Keep a daily log documenting: opening/closing temperatures, cleaning completion times, any error codes, product refills, and maintenance calls. This log helps IT support diagnose recurring issues and supports food safety audits. Your manager should review logs weekly.
