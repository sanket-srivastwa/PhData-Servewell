# Online Orders — Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**Q: How do I pause accepting orders on ServeWell Online Portal?**
A: Log into ServeWell Online Portal v3.x with your manager credentials and navigate to **Settings > Order Status**. Toggle the "Accept Orders" switch to OFF. Orders will stop flowing immediately, but you can resume at any time by toggling back ON. This affects all connected platforms (Zomato and Swiggy) simultaneously.

**Q: What's the difference between pausing orders and going offline?**
A: Pausing orders keeps your restaurant visible on Zomato and Swiggy but stops new orders from being accepted—useful during prep shortages. Going offline removes your restaurant from search results entirely—use this only for extended closures. Pause is faster and more flexible for temporary issues.

**Q: How long does it take for a pause to sync across Zomato and Swiggy?**
A: Changes sync within 2–5 minutes. If orders continue arriving after 5 minutes, check your internet connection and manually refresh the portal. If the issue persists, contact L2 support immediately.

**Q: Can I pause orders for just one delivery platform?**
A: No. The ServeWell Portal pause function affects all connected platforms at once. To pause only Zomato or Swiggy, you must log into that platform's restaurant dashboard directly and pause there.

**Q: What should I do if I receive a duplicate order?**
A: Do not process both orders. Check the order ID and timestamp in ServeWell Portal to identify which is the duplicate (usually a few seconds apart). Contact the customer via the original order's contact details to confirm which order they intended, then cancel the duplicate immediately in the portal with the note "Duplicate order—customer confirmed original order only."

## Troubleshooting

**Q: What counts as a sync failure?**
A: A sync failure occurs when an order appears on Zomato or Swiggy but does NOT appear in ServeWell Portal within 3 minutes, OR when you accept an order in ServeWell but it doesn't update status on the delivery platform within 2 minutes. Document the order ID, timestamp, and platform, then escalate to L2.

**Q: Orders are showing in ServeWell but not on Zomato—what do I do?**
A: First, refresh the Zomato app or website (hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R on desktop). Wait 2 minutes and check again. If the order still doesn't appear, verify your internet connection and check that Zomato integration is active in ServeWell (**Settings > Integrations**). If integration shows "Disconnected," reconnect it using your Zomato merchant credentials.

**Q: An order arrived on Zomato but disappeared from ServeWell Portal—what happened?**
A: This typically indicates a sync failure or connection drop. Check your internet connection immediately. Log out and back into ServeWell Portal. If the order reappears, the issue has resolved. If it's still missing after 5 minutes, take a screenshot of the Zomato order and escalate to L2 with the order ID and timestamp.

**Q: How do I know if Zomato or Swiggy integration has disconnected?**
A: In ServeWell Portal, go to **Settings > Integrations** and check the status next to each platform. A green "Connected" badge means active; red "Disconnected" means the link is broken. Disconnected integrations will not receive orders. Reconnect by clicking the platform name and re-entering your merchant credentials.

**Q: Orders are arriving very slowly—is this a system issue?**
A: Slow order arrival (>5 minutes delay) can indicate network congestion, high platform load, or integration lag. Check your internet speed (minimum 5 Mbps recommended). Verify both Zomato and Swiggy integrations are showing "Connected" in ServeWell Settings. If speeds are normal and integrations are connected, the delay is likely platform-side; monitor for 10 minutes. If delays persist longer than 30 minutes, contact L2.

## When to Call IT Support

**Q: When should I escalate a Zomato issue to Zomato support vs. ServeWell IT?**
A: Escalate to **Zomato support** (Zomato Partner Helpline: 1-800-ZOMATO-1 or partner support portal) for issues with the Zomato platform itself—visibility, order visibility to customers, or Zomato app crashes. Escalate to **ServeWell IT** for issues within ServeWell Portal or if you suspect an integration problem between ServeWell and Zomato.

**Q: When should I escalate a Swiggy issue to Swiggy support vs. ServeWell IT?**
A: Contact **Swiggy Partner Support** (Swiggy Business Partner Line: 1-800-SWIGGY-1 or partner dashboard support) for Swiggy platform issues—visibility, app problems, or account access. Contact **ServeWell IT** if orders appear in Swiggy but not in ServeWell, or if the integration is disconnected.

**Q: When do I need to raise an IT support ticket instead of calling the helpdesk?**
A: Raise a ticket (via ServeWell Portal **Help > Support Tickets**) for non-urgent issues (expected response: 4–24 hours) such as historical order data queries, feature requests, or documentation issues. Call the helpdesk directly for urgent issues impacting current operations—sync failures, widespread order loss, or integration disconnections. For urgent issues, call first and create a ticket as backup documentation.

**Q: What information should I provide when reporting an order sync issue?**
A: Provide the order ID, timestamp (exact time order appeared), which platform it came from (Zomato/Swiggy), which platform it's missing from, your store location, and a screenshot if possible. Also note your internet connection status and whether other orders are syncing normally. This speeds up troubleshooting significantly.
