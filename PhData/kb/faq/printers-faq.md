# Printers — Frequently Asked Problems

## General

**Q: What type of paper should I use in the EpsonTM-T88VI printer?**
A: Use only 80mm thermal receipt paper (80×80mm rolls) with a 12mm core. Do not use plain copy paper, as it will damage the thermal head and void the warranty. Check the packaging to ensure the paper is specifically rated for thermal printers. Store paper in a cool, dry place to prevent curling.

**Q: Where do I find the printer model number?**
A: The model number is printed on a label on the back or bottom of the device. For the EpsonTM-T88VI, it will clearly display "TM-T88VI." You'll need this information when contacting IT support or ordering supplies.

**Q: How often should I replace the paper roll?**
A: Replace the paper roll when it runs out or when the print quality becomes faint. A standard 80×80mm roll typically lasts 4–8 hours depending on transaction volume. Keep spare rolls in stock to avoid service interruptions.

**Q: What is StarMC Print v2?**
A: StarMC Print v2 is the driver software that enables communication between your POS system and the printer. It handles print jobs, formatting, and device settings. You should not uninstall or modify this software without IT approval.

## Troubleshooting

**Q: The printer isn't printing. What should I do first?**
A: Check that the paper roll is loaded correctly and not jammed, and verify the printer is powered on and connected to the network. Restart the printer by turning it off for 10 seconds, then back on. If the issue persists, check the StarMC Print v2 print queue on your POS terminal—if jobs are stuck, restart the POS system and call the helpdesk.

**Q: Paper is jammed inside the printer. How do I safely clear it?**
A: Power off the printer immediately. Open the paper compartment door on the front and gently pull out any visible paper fragments using slow, steady pressure—do not yank or force removal. Check the thermal head area for torn pieces and carefully remove them with a soft, dry cloth. If paper is wrapped around internal components or the jam doesn't clear easily, **do not force it**—stop and contact IT support to prevent damage.

**Q: Why is the print quality faint or faded?**
A: This usually means the paper roll is nearly empty or the thermal head needs cleaning. Replace the paper roll first. If quality remains poor after installing new paper, the printer may need calibration or cleaning—contact IT support. Do not attempt to clean the thermal head yourself, as oils from your skin can damage it.

**Q: The printer is making unusual noises or grinding sounds.**
A: Power off the printer immediately and check for jammed paper or torn fragments inside. Remove any obstructions carefully. If no jam is visible or noises continue after clearing, **do not use the printer**—report it to IT support right away, as internal mechanical damage may have occurred.

**Q: Print jobs are queuing but nothing is printing.**
A: Check that the printer is online and powered on. Look at the StarMC Print v2 status on your POS terminal—if it shows "Offline" or "Error," restart the printer and your POS system. If the queue still doesn't clear, right-click the printer in your system settings and select "Clear Print Queue," then restart the POS. If this doesn't resolve it, contact IT support.

**Q: The printer is offline on the network.**
A: Verify the ethernet or USB cable is firmly connected to both the printer and your terminal. Restart the printer by powering it off for 10 seconds. Check that your POS terminal is connected to the network. If the printer remains offline, note the IP address (visible on the printer's network settings display) and contact IT support with this information.

## Driver & Updates

**Q: When will StarMC Print v2 be updated?**
A: IT will announce driver updates via email and in the helpdesk portal. **Do not install updates yourself.** Updates will be deployed by IT during scheduled maintenance windows to ensure compatibility with your POS system. Check your email and the knowledge base for notifications.

**Q: My printer driver seems outdated. Should I update it?**
A: Do not manually update drivers. If you suspect an outdated driver is causing issues, contact IT support and reference the current version number shown in StarMC Print v2 settings (usually found in Device Properties or About). IT will assess whether an update is needed and deploy it safely.

## When to Call IT Support

**Q: When should I contact IT instead of troubleshooting myself?**
A: Contact IT support immediately if: the printer won't power on, there is visible damage or unusual grinding sounds, paper is jammed and won't clear safely, print quality doesn't improve after replacing paper, the printer remains offline after a restart, or print jobs consistently fail after clearing the queue. Do not attempt repairs yourself.

**Q: Should I replace or repair a broken printer?**
A: IT will determine whether repair or replacement is more cost-effective. For EpsonTM-T88VI units under 3 years old with isolated issues (like paper feed problems or driver conflicts), repair is usually the first option. For devices older than 5 years, with multiple mechanical failures, or extensive damage, replacement may be recommended. Contact IT support with the model number and a description of the problem.

**Q: What information should I provide when reporting a printer issue?**
A: Provide the printer model (e.g., EpsonTM-T88VI), location/store, a clear description of the problem, steps you've already taken, and any error messages you see. If the printer is offline, note the IP address from the network settings display. This information helps IT resolve the issue faster.
